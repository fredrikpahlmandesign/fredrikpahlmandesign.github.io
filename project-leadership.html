Since 2017, I've been Chief Design Officer and Head of Directors at inUse. As part of the executive management team, I'm responsible for inUse's consultant capabilities — skills development, competence screening in recruitment, work process development, coaching, and more — for the 100+ designers at the company.

I do it with the help of my team of Directors, pictured below.

My team of Directors at inUse.

Earlier — UX Director

A few years before, inUse needed senior designers to spend half their time improving our craft and making sure we delivered with excellence. I was one of the first two picked for that role, and the group grew over time.

The work included planning and often performing skills development classes for colleagues in Gothenburg, Stockholm and Malmö, coaching designers in projects, supporting project deliveries and client presentations, and helping out in sales meetings with clients.

At the same time, I was a teacher for inUse's "UX in Practice" class — running two-day courses in Stockholm and Gothenburg for clients and competitors alike, in between assignments. I also spoke at the Stockholm Control Room Conference, and was stage moderator for the inUse Award.
